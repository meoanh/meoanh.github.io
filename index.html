Desktop Tools

Small Windows tools made by one developer for a group of friends and colleagues. Each one runs on your own PC. None of them has a server that receives your Google data.

My Drive Syncer

Uploads and keeps local folders in sync with your Google Drive. It needs access to the Drive files it uploads and updates.

Direct Stream

Plays music and video stored in your Google Drive without downloading whole folders first. It only reads your Drive; it never changes or deletes anything.

Sync GSheet and Excel

Copies chosen ranges between Excel workbooks on your PC and your Google Sheets, and installs a small Apps Script menu in the spreadsheet you choose.

Privacy policy

Công cụ Windows nhỏ cho bạn bè và đồng nghiệp. Mọi dữ liệu Google chỉ được xử lý trên máy của bạn.
