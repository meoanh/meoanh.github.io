Privacy Policy

Applies to My Drive Syncer, Direct Stream and Sync GSheet and Excel. Last updated 2026-11-22.

What the apps access

When you sign in with Google, each app asks only for the access it needs:

  • My Drive Syncer: your Google Drive files, to upload and update the folders you choose.
  • Direct Stream: read-only access to your Google Drive files and their metadata, to list and play your media.
  • Sync GSheet and Excel: the Google Sheets you work with, files the app creates or opens, and Apps Script projects, to copy data and install the spreadsheet menu.

Where your data goes

The apps run entirely on your own computer. Your Google data travels only between your computer and Google. It is not sent to the developer, is not stored on any server of ours, and is never sold or shared.

The sign-in token is saved on your computer so you do not have to sign in every time. Delete the app, or remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the access ends.

Log files you choose to send

If an app fails, it may offer to send its log files to the developer. Nothing is sent unless you confirm. Logs describe what the app did (for example error messages and file names); they never contain your Google password or sign-in token.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The apps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen of the app.
